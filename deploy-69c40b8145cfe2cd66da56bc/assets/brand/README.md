# BBCI brand assets

The BBCI mark uses an accurate Wisconsin boundary filled with four flat landscape bands: sky, field, water, and forest. The narrow water band stays horizontal at every size.

## Files

- `bbci-mark.svg` - primary standalone mark
- `bbci-logo-horizontal.svg` - preferred full-name lockup
- `bbci-logo-stacked.svg` - centered stacked lockup
- `bbci-logo-horizontal-reverse.svg` - light wordmark for dark backgrounds
- `bbci-mark-16.png`, `bbci-mark-32.png`, `bbci-mark-180.png`, `bbci-mark-192.png`, `bbci-mark-512.png`, `bbci-mark-1024.png` - favicon, app icon, and general PNG exports
- `bbci-logo-horizontal-1600.png`, `bbci-logo-stacked-1200.png`, `bbci-logo-horizontal-reverse-1600.png` - presentation and office-document PNG exports

## Core colors

- Sky: `#D9BE83`
- Field: `#789568`
- Water: `#4E8794`
- Forest: `#28563C`
- Wordmark: `#193A27`

Maintain clear space equal to one quarter of the mark width. Do not use gradients, textures, drop the water band, reorder the colors, distort the Wisconsin silhouette, or place the mark in a pill or bubble.
